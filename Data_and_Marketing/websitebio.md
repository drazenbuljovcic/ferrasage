Short Bio:
Driven by curiosity and a tendency to challenge established solutions, Dalibor Buljovčić
built his career around complex electronics, power conversion and magnetic design.
Experience spanning hardware architecture, EMC, industrialization and supplier
collaboration gradually shaped a broader system-level perspective. He founded
Ferrasage to bring that thinking into a company focused on electrification architecture
and advanced magnetic solutions.
Long Bio:
I’ve always been more interested in understanding why something works than simply
accepting that it does.
That curiosity has followed me throughout my engineering career, from electronics and
embedded hardware to power converters, EMC, magnetic components and eventually
complete system architecture. Most important thing those experiences taught me was to
stop seeing engineering disciplines in isolation, as most interesting engineering
problems appear at the boundaries between.
Power electronics became a particular passion, especially magnetic components. The
deeper I went into technology, the more interested I became in the bigger picture around
it. Working on increasingly complex products gave me today’s engineering perspective:
instead of focusing only on individual components, I became increasingly interested in
understanding the complete system and the decisions that shape it.
I also discovered that I enjoy challenging existing solutions. Improvement starts with
stepping back, questioning the original assumptions and looking at the problem from
another angle.
Ferrasage is where those threads come together.
It is place where magnetics, electrification and multidisciplinary engineering live.
